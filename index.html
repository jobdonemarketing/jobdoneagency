Job Done Agency provides AI-assisted, human-verified scouting to licensed football agents and clubs. Our pilot programme covers young attackers and midfielders aged 16 to 18 in Romania.

What we offer

We are a scouting and research service. We identify promising young players, then prepare clear dossiers for our clients, licensed agents and clubs, on an exclusive basis. We do not represent players or negotiate transfers.

Open search

We identify the strongest young attackers and midfielders in our coverage area and present them to you first.

Targeted search

You describe the exact profile a team needs, such as role, style, traits and age, and we search specifically for players who fit.

Player dossiers

Each candidate comes with a one-page summary, a short curated video reel and a background note on club, level and availability.

Exclusivity

At any given time, every candidate is presented to one agent only. We do not offer the same player to competing agencies.

Pilot coverage: Romania

We start where we can deliver the most value: young football talent in Romanian county and zonal competitions, where structured data and international attention are limited. If the collaboration proves worthwhile, we plan to expand to other EU countries and later beyond.

How it works

A simple, transparent process from first contact to delivery.

  1. Brief

    Tell us whether you want an open search or a targeted search, and describe the profile you need.

  2. Search

    Our team searches and reviews candidates using AI-assisted analysis and human verification.

  3. Delivery

    You receive a dossier on each candidate, exclusively. Details of a minor are shared only after written consent from the player's parents or legal guardians.

  4. Your next step

    Any contact with a player or his family, and any representation agreement, is a matter between you and them under the applicable regulations.

Our principles

Working with information about young people carries responsibility. These are the standards we hold ourselves to.

Honesty

We give candid assessments. We do not make promises about contracts, transfers or outcomes, because no one can guarantee them.

Protection of minors

Players under 18 are presented only with the written consent of a parent or legal guardian, and their data is handled in line with applicable data-protection rules.

Transparency

The terms of any collaboration, including fees and exclusivity, are explained clearly and agreed in writing.

Respect for the sport

We operate within the rules of the relevant governing bodies and the regulations on the movement of young players.

Job Done Agency is a scouting and research service. It does not represent players, negotiate transfers or guarantee placement, selection or professional contracts.
