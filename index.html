What we do

We act as scouts on behalf of amateur athletes. Our role is to identify promising talent, prepare it to be seen, and open conversations with professional clubs, teams, academies and federations across borders.

Talent identification

We review the profile, results and potential of young amateurs to understand where they stand and what level they can realistically aim for.

Professional pathways

We map the routes into professional sport in each discipline and country, from academies and trials to developmental and senior contracts.

International introductions

We connect athletes with clubs, teams and organizations worldwide, so talent is not limited by where it happens to be born.

Guidance for families

We explain each step in plain language to athletes and their parents or guardians, so decisions are made with clear information.

Any official sport, worldwide

We are not limited to a single discipline or region. Our scope covers any sport officially recognized by its international or national governing body, whether individual or team-based, Olympic or not.

Who we work with

  • Young amateur athletes aiming to turn professional.
  • Parents and guardians supporting a young athlete's career.
  • Coaches and clubs that want to see their players reach the next level.

Who we speak to

  • Professional clubs and teams.
  • Academies and development programs.
  • Leagues, federations and sports organizations.

How it works

A simple, transparent process from first contact to introductions.

  1. Contact us

    Send us an email with the athlete's name, age, sport, country, current club or team, and a short summary of their results and goals.

  2. Initial review

    Our scouts assess the information provided and reply with an honest view of the athlete's situation and the next steps, if any.

  3. Evaluation

    Where there is potential, we gather further information such as match or competition footage, statistics and references to build a complete profile.

  4. Introductions

    We present the athlete to suitable professional organizations and support the process while opportunities are explored.

Our principles

Working with young people carries responsibility. These are the standards we hold ourselves to.

Honesty

We give candid assessments. We do not make promises about contracts or outcomes, because no one can guarantee them.

Protection of minors

Athletes under 18 are engaged with the knowledge and involvement of a parent or legal guardian.

Transparency

Terms of any collaboration are explained clearly and in writing before anything is agreed.

Respect for the sport

We operate within the rules of the relevant governing bodies and the regulations on the movement of young athletes.

Job Done Agency is a scouting and representation service. It does not guarantee placement, selection or professional contracts.
